01 / About

An analyst who understands the question behind the numbers.

My work sits at the intersection of quantitative analysis, business research and communication. I care about what happened, what is changing, why it matters, and what action could follow.

“Data has value when it leads to understanding, and understanding has value when it supports better decisions.”

I’m James Andanje, a Data Analyst and Research & Assistant Consultant. My foundation is a BSc in Statistics and Programming, strengthened by practical work across Power BI, Excel, Power Query, DAX, market research, business research, strategic analysis and executive communication.

Rather than treating analytics as an isolated technical task, I connect the full analytical journey — from the business question and source data to interpretation, visualization and decision support.

I also bring strong information-management, stakeholder-communication and presentation skills. Those capabilities help me organize evidence carefully, explain findings clearly and design analytical outputs around the people who need to use them.
